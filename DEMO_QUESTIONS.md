# Besswave Onboarding Demo Questions

Source: `demo.ts`

## Identity

1. What's your name, title, and company?
2. Who do you report to, and who are your direct reports?
3. What enterprise systems do you work in daily? Name them.

## Priorities

4. What are the 2-3 big-picture priorities for your team this year?

## Goals

5. Your most important measurable goal right now - what's the target and deadline?
6. One more active goal you're tracking?
7. Any wins you've already completed this year worth noting?

## Decisions

8. What are the 2-3 operational decisions you make most often? What data drives each one?

## Friction

9. What was the most painful data-gathering workflow before Besswave? Describe the old process and what it cost you.

## Preferences

10. How do you want answers delivered? What should Besswave always or never do?
