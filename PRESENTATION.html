User Context System

How the AI learns who you are —
and why it changes everything.

Powered by structured user context  ·  Inspired by the Telos pattern

The Problem

Without context, every query starts from zero.

Without context
You: How are we doing on open IT tickets?
I don't have access to your ticketing data. Could you tell me which system you use? And what metrics would you like to see — volume, resolution time, open count?

Every. Single. Query. The AI asks clarifying questions instead of answering. Context resets to zero every time.

The Fix

With context, the AI already knows you.

With context (Sarah Chen loaded)
You: How are we doing on open IT tickets?
Your current open ticket SLA is 3.8 days against your Q2 target of 2.5 days — you're off track by 1.3 days. ServiceNow shows 147 open P2+ tickets, up 23% from last month. The Portland site accounts for 31 of those, which is disproportionate to its headcount ratio. Want me to break that down by category or team member?

One file. Prepended to every query. The AI knows the system, the target, the anomaly — and what to surface next.

The Integration Point

CONTEXT_SUMMARY.md

~400 tokens · prepended to every query · auto-regenerated when source files change

You are assisting Sarah Chen, VP of Operations at MidWest Distribution Co (Indianapolis, IN — ~450 employees, ~$65M revenue). She manages IT operations, facilities, and process improvement across three sites, reporting to COO Linda Garrison.
What she's driving right now:
· IT ticket SLA from 4.2 days to 2.5 days by September 30 (currently at 3.8 days — off track)
· Facilities maintenance backlog from 62 open items to fewer than 20 by July 15
· Chicago device refresh: 34 of 85 devices complete, deadline June 30
Connected systems: ServiceNow · Salesforce · Microsoft Intune · NetSuite · Snowflake
How to answer her: Lead with the number and whether it's on track or off track. Flag anomalies in the first sentence. Use system names. Use tables for comparisons with 3+ categories.

This is what changes everything.

Roadmap

The pattern is proven. Here's what comes next.

1
Onboarding interview UI — Guided conversation that captures the six phases and writes the context files
2
CONTEXT_SUMMARY regenerator — Auto-synthesize whenever a source file changes
3
Per-user secure storage — Context files contain sensitive operational data; store accordingly
4
Refresh triggers — Quarterly prompts to review goals; auto-regenerate on integration changes
5
Preference learning — In-session feedback ("less detail please") updates PREFERENCES.md automatically
